## What I am explicitly NOT claiming

More trades ≠ more edge. The screen found 34 qualifiers from 919 tests where
chance alone would produce ~46 at p<0.05. **Widening the book buys statistical
resolution, not alpha.** It shortens the time to know whether an edge exists
from ~15 years toward ~7; it does not make one exist. If the edge is truly
zero, this book will now demonstrate that faster and more expensively.
